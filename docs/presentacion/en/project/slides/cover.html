Team Los AstroCoders

NASA Space Apps 2026

Hi, we are Los AstroCoders, and this is SONORA, our project for The Earth Information Jukebox challenge. NASA’s visualizations are some of the best tools for understanding the planet, but almost all of them depend on sight: colors, legends and animations. A blind or low-vision person gets, at best, one sentence of alt text. We wanted anyone to be able to listen to the planet and explore the whole dataset.
