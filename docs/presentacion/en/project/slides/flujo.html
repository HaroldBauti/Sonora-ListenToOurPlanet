HOW IT WORKS

From image to music in five steps

1

Source

A NASA visualization, your own image, or a video of 16 to 64 frames.

2

Layers

4 color families: the official palette or k-means.

3

Sound

Each layer becomes melody, rhythm, bass or harmony.

4

NSGA-II

Balances fidelity and musicality. You pick your point on the Pareto front.

5

Sweep

Linear from west to east, or radar, with sparkles and 8 scales.

The music engine exists in both C# and JavaScript. Every front that loads is checked to make sure both produce the same music.

SONORA · NASA Space Apps 2026

First we take the source: a NASA visualization, an image or a video. We split its colors into four layers, using the official palette when we know it, or k-means when we don’t. Each layer is an instrument. An evolutionary algorithm, NSGA-II, searches for the rules that best balance fidelity to the data and musicality, and the user picks their own balance point. Finally, the image is swept from west to east, or like a radar, just like NASA’s Caldwell 73 sonification.
