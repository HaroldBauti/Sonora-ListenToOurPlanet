The whole page by voice

It greets you and asks whether to turn on hands-free mode. Then it listens, answers and reads the page in order, saying what each thing is: section, tab, button or checkbox.

Try saying

“read the page” · “next section” · “open the climate tab” · “play number 3” · “press download video”

English or Spanish, following the browser · Chrome and Edge

Accessibility is the core of the project. Everything works with the keyboard, and the arrow keys let you explore the map like a grid where every cell plays a sound and gets described. There is screen reader support, narration, vibration on Android and visual options. We also designed for a blind person opening the page for the first time: SONORA greets them by voice, asks whether they want hands-free mode, and from there they can read the page, jump between sections, open tabs or press buttons just by speaking.
